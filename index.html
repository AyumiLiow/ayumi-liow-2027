what is 感性 kansei?

kansei is the human sensibility behind our impressions. why does some music give us pleasure? why do we feel a sunset is beautiful?

it is hard to define, and closely tied to personality and humanness. the matsui lab studies it through cognitive science, psychology, statistics and ai.

Source: MATSUI Tatsunori Laboratory, School of Human Sciences, Waseda University
